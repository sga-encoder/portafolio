I built this blog for a university research group as a way to publish and showcase its progress. The
frontend is built with Next.js and the content is managed from a Strapi backend/CMS
([backend repo](https://github.com/sga-encoder/blog-semillero-strapi)).

Right now the demo shows sample data, not the research group's actual progress yet.
