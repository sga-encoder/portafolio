Territorial is a territorial valuation system I built for university. I built the frontend in Angular
with Tailwind CSS: interactive maps with MapLibre to delimit neighborhoods by drawing their polygons and
to track field staff in real time, plus management of departments, cities, districts, entities and
users. The backend was built by the university.

It has three roles (administrator, field staff and citizen), and new accounts stay pending until an
administrator assigns them a role.
